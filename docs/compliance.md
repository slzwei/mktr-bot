# Voice marketing consent and DNC checks

Every outbound dial, including the simulator console and campaign retries, must have stored evidence of clear consent for MKTR voice marketing or a fresh result showing the Singapore number is absent from the No Voice Call Register. CSV import alone grants no permission. Operator-created records must identify the source, time and evidence reference; simulator fixtures are not production consent.

This application uses a **21-day** validity limit for DNC results, measured from receipt of the Registry result. This is stricter than the checklist's maximum 30 days and follows the current [PDPC DNC Registry Business Rules](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/overview-of-pdpa/do-not-call-registry/business-owner/do-not-call-registry-business-rules). An expired result, future timestamp, unavailable record or negative result blocks dialing unless valid consent exists. A recorded opt-out blocks dialing immediately even if an earlier DNC result is clear. Non-Singapore numbers require recorded consent; a Singapore Registry result cannot clear them.

The legal basis used is clear and unambiguous consent in an accessible evidential form, or checking the applicable DNC register within its validity period. The software does not infer an ongoing relationship, B2B exception, or another exemption from contact names or imported data. [PDPC's guidance for businesses](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/overview-of-pdpa/do-not-call-registry/business-owner/do-not-call-registry-and-your-business) describes the consent exception, visible caller identity and voice opt-out requirements.

Shawn chooses and registers any DNC account and performs or arranges Registry checks. Record the actual result, receipt time and reference in the application before dialing; the software does not purchase checks or manufacture clearances. Keep the underlying consent/Registry evidence in MKTR's controlled records. Review the opening clip so it identifies MKTR, states the purpose and offers a voice opt-out. Honor withdrawal immediately in the application. Campaign contacts show a skip reason when permission is missing or has expired.
