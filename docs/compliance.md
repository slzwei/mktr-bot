# Voice marketing consent and DNC checks

Every outbound dial, including the simulator console and campaign retries, must have stored evidence of clear consent for MKTR voice marketing or a fresh result showing the Singapore number is absent from the No Voice Call Register. CSV import alone grants no permission. Operator-created records must identify the source, time and evidence reference; simulator fixtures are not production consent.

This application uses a **21-day** validity limit for DNC results, measured from receipt of the Registry result. This is stricter than the checklist's maximum 30 days and follows the current [PDPC DNC Registry Business Rules](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/overview-of-pdpa/do-not-call-registry/business-owner/do-not-call-registry-business-rules). An expired result, future timestamp, unavailable record or negative result blocks dialing unless valid consent exists. A recorded opt-out blocks dialing immediately even if an earlier DNC result is clear. Non-Singapore numbers require recorded consent; a Singapore Registry result cannot clear them.

The legal basis used is clear and unambiguous consent in an accessible evidential form, or checking the applicable DNC register within its validity period. The software does not infer an ongoing relationship, B2B exception, or another exemption from contact names or imported data. [PDPC's guidance for businesses](https://www.pdpc.gov.sg/overview-of-pdpa/do-not-call-registry/business-owner/do-not-call-registry-and-your-business) describes the consent exception, visible caller identity and voice opt-out requirements.

Shawn chooses and registers the DNC account, arranges its prepaid credits and provisions the mktr-platform gateway. Automatic checking ships disabled. When enabled, the application submits new Singapore voice numbers through that gateway in batches of at most 100 after a successful contact import. It omits duplicates, numbers with valid recorded consent and numbers with a Registry result still within 21 days, including a negative result. The free CSV preview shows the credit count before the operator clicks the import button; a separate action in Voice call permission states its one-credit cost and checks one Singapore number through the same module. Viewing permission, previewing, selecting contacts and starting a campaign do not purchase Registry checks. There is no automatic expiry refresh or paid retry.

Only a complete, validated PDPC S000 batch response creates automatic DNC evidence. Each record uses the response receipt time for `checkedAt`, the Registry transaction ID as its reference, and the No Voice Call Register flag for its voice decision; text or fax registration alone does not block voice calls. The original creation time, validity date and all three register flags remain in the append-only JSON snapshot as evidence. The existing 21-day local limit and opt-out precedence continue to govern every dial. A failed, partial or ambiguous batch writes no result for that batch, stops further checks and leaves the successful import intact; earlier complete S000 batches retain their evidence. A lost reply may still have spent credits and is never retried automatically.

The operator UI shows imported and duplicate counts, numbers checked, clear and registered results, already-covered and non-Singapore counts, and any failure cause. Contact rows show permission or the blocking reason before campaign selection, with Singapore expiry dates when clearance expires within three days; campaign detail summarizes who is dialable now and why others are blocked. Manual consent, opt-out and DNC evidence entry all remain available, including results obtained outside this system and consent for non-Singapore numbers. Record the actual result, receipt time and reference when entering external evidence. Keep the underlying consent/Registry evidence in MKTR's controlled records.
